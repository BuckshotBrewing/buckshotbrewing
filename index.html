The brand

For the part of the day everyone remembers.

Buckshot is not trying to be a taproom brand or a complicated craft-beer lecture. It is a Minnesota lager brand built around identity, loyalty, and the outdoor occasions where domestic light beer already wins.

The design language is confident and established: deep red, black, brass, forest green, and cream used with restraint. The result should feel premium on a retail shelf and natural in a truck bed, cabin fridge, or neighborhood bar.

Where it belongs

Made for the way Minnesota gets outside.

Hunting, fishing, cabins, campfires, tailgates, golf, fish houses, and weekends with friends. The imagery supports the beer instead of turning the brand into a gimmick.
