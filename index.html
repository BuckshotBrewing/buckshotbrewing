Why Buckshot

Made for the story after the day.

Buckshot is built around the moments when the work is done and the best part starts: the cabin fridge, the truck bed, the neighborhood bar, the golf cooler, and the fire after dark.

The brand is confident, welcoming, and unmistakably Minnesotan. It is not trying to impress beer nerds. It is trying to earn a regular spot with people who know exactly what they like.
