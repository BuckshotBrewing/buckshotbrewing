Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Information you provide

When you contact Buckshot Brewing or request launch updates, you may provide information such as your name, email address, company, and message.

Shopify and merchandise purchases

Our merchandise store, checkout, and related customer service are handled through Shopify. Information you provide while shopping or contacting us through Shopify may be collected and processed by Shopify according to its services and privacy practices.

How we use information

We may use information to respond to inquiries, support merchandise orders, share updates you request, improve the customer experience, and operate the Buckshot Brewing business.

Marketing choices

We will send marketing emails only when you have agreed to receive them. You can use the unsubscribe option in a marketing email or contact us to update your preferences.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers, such as Shopify, when needed to operate the website, process merchandise orders, or respond to requests.

Questions

Email sales@buckshotbrewing.com.

This plain-language policy should be reviewed by counsel as Buckshot Brewing's products, marketing tools, and operations evolve.
